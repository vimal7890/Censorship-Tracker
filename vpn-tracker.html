Service availability by country

The 15 most widely used consumer VPN providers, checked against every jurisdiction in the table above: can you actually use the service there. This tracks the connection rather than the app listing — Pakistan, for one, leaves the apps in both stores and blocks the tunnels instead. It also separates the two ways a service can be unusable: blocked means the state blocks it, unavailable means the provider itself withdrew from the market. Where a country restricts circumvention tools wholesale rather than by name, the country baseline applies; providers named in reporting override it.

✓Available Works normally !Restricted Degraded, licensed-only or legally risky ✕Blocked Blocked by the state ⊘Unavailable Withdrawn by the provider

Blocked does not mean unreachable. These ratings describe a service’s official standing and what happens on a default connection — not the ceiling on what is technically possible. Unavailable is a different problem with a different fix: the provider has withdrawn from that market, so the obstacle is buying and installing the service rather than connecting through a filter. Obfuscation features that disguise VPN traffic as ordinary HTTPS, bridge servers, and self-hosted tunnels still get people online across every jurisdiction listed here, which is precisely why these regimes keep expanding their filtering. The more useful warning is legal rather than technical: in Myanmar, Iran, Turkmenistan and Belarus the act of circumventing the block is itself an offence, and enforcement has extended to on-the-spot phone searches. Where that is the case, the risk of getting around a block is not a failed connection.

Apple iCloud Private Relay Restrictions

Apple Feature Index

iCloud Private Relay Regional Availability

iCloud Private Relay is Apple’s dual-hop privacy feature designed to encrypt Safari browsing traffic and obscure user IP addresses. Apple disables or omits Private Relay in jurisdictions with strict internet surveillance laws or regulatory requirements, as well as regions where paid iCloud+ services are not supported.
