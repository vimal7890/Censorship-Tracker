Service availability by country

The 15 most widely used consumer VPN providers, checked against every jurisdiction in the table above: can you actually use the service there. This tracks the connection rather than the app listing — Pakistan, for one, leaves the apps in both stores and blocks the tunnels instead. Where a country restricts circumvention tools wholesale rather than by name, the country baseline applies; providers named in reporting override it.

✓Available Works normally !Restricted Degraded, licensed-only or legally risky ✕Blocked Does not work without circumvention

Blocked does not mean unreachable. These ratings describe a service’s official standing and what happens on a default connection — not the ceiling on what is technically possible. Obfuscation features that disguise VPN traffic as ordinary HTTPS, bridge servers, and self-hosted tunnels still get people online across every jurisdiction listed here, which is precisely why these regimes keep expanding their filtering. The more useful warning is legal rather than technical: in Myanmar, Iran, Turkmenistan and Belarus the act of circumventing the block is itself an offence, and enforcement has extended to on-the-spot phone searches. Where that is the case, the risk of getting around a block is not a failed connection.
