Blocked in Ukraine

The tracker lists 5 complete bans and 1 partial restriction in Ukraine.

Includes state-level jurisdiction: Crimea.

Open the interactive dossier → Follow changes in Ukraine (RSS)

Netflix

Complete ban Since: 2016 (U.S. sanctions) In force Crimea Netflix help centre

Netflix has never been available in Crimea. When it expanded nearly worldwide in January 2016, Crimea was excluded alongside Syria, North Korea, and People's Republic of China due to U.S. government trade sanctions prohibiting U.S. companies from providing services to the region.

Odnoklassniki

Complete ban Since: 2017 Reported Human Rights Watch

Blocked by the Ukrainian government via ISP bans under Presidential Decree No. 133/2017 targeting Russian tech companies alongside VKontakte and other Russian services.

Russia Today

Complete ban Since: August 2014 Reported Freedom House

Banned alongside other Russian state media after the annexation of Crimea and blocked at ISP level under sanctions decrees renewed since 2017. OONI recorded anomalies or confirmed blockpages in roughly 85% of Ukrainian measurements of rt.com.

VKontakte

Complete ban Since: 2017 Reported Human Rights Watch

Blocked by the Ukrainian government via ISP bans under Presidential Decree No. 133/2017 targeting Russian tech companies.

Yandex

Complete ban Since: 2017 Reported Baker McKenzie sanctions news

Blocked via ISP-level restrictions since May 2017 under a Poroshenko-era NSDC decree; Ukraine's NSDC most recently renewed the sanctions for a further 10 years under Presidential Decree No. 227/2023 (April 2023), keeping yandex.ru and yandex.ua inaccessible.

GitHub

Partial restriction Since: 2019 In force Crimea GitHub docs

GitHub restricts access to private repositories and commercial services for developers in the Crimea region to comply with US sanctions.
