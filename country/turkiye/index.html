Blocked in Türkiye

The tracker lists 4 complete bans, 1 partial restriction and 9 age-verification requirements in Türkiye.

Open the interactive dossier → Follow changes in Türkiye (RSS)

Deutsche Welle

Complete ban Since: June 2022 Reported Committee to Protect Journalists

Türkiye's media regulator RTÜK blocked access to dw.com in June 2022 after Deutsche Welle declined to apply for a domestic online broadcast licence.

Discord

Complete ban Since: October 2024 Reported Türkiye Today

An Ankara court blocked Discord in October 2024 over child-safety concerns. As of June 2026 it remains blocked — ministers say Discord has met compliance criteria, but a fresh court ruling is required to restore access.

Grindr

Complete ban Since: August 2013 In force Grindr support

Banned by court order in August 2013. Grindr's official Help Center lists Türkiye as a government-restricted country.

Roblox

Complete ban Since: August 2024 Reported Reuters

Blocked by an Adana court order over child exploitation concerns and inappropriate content.

X

Partial restriction Since: July 2025 Reported Balkan Insight

A Turkish court ordered blocks on content from X's Grok chatbot in July 2025 after it generated posts insulting President Erdogan.

Facebook

Age verification Since: late 2026 Reported Al Jazeera

Türkiye's parliament passed a law in April 2026 banning social media services for children under 15 and requiring platforms to implement age-verification systems and parental control tools for older minors. The law takes effect six months after publication in the Official Gazette, with full compliance expected in late 2026. A separate e-government (e-Devlet) real-ID login scheme for all users was still a draft as of April 2026.
