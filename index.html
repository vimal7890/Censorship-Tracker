Methodology & Sources

What this tracks

Apps and websites that a government or the platform itself has made unavailable or conditional in a given territory. Every entry is one of three kinds:

  • Complete — the platform is fully blocked.
  • Partial — throttled, feature-limited or intermittently blocked.
  • Age verification — legal age-gating required to access it.

How sources are held

Every claim cites a high-quality primary or reputable secondary source — government decrees, OONI and Citizen Lab measurements, Freedom House research, established newsrooms, or a platform's own transparency pages. Wikipedia is not used except for the handful of countries whose internet is restricted by default.

Each link is opened and confirmed to support its specific row — a 200 is never treated as proof on its own — and sources are never invented or auto-substituted. verify_links.py re-checks every URL for rot.
