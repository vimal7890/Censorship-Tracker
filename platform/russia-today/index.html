Where is Russia Today blocked?

Russia Today is blocked, restricted or age-gated in 37 countries and territories — 34 complete bans and 3 partial restrictions in total.

Albania

Complete ban Since: 2022 Reported

As countries closely aligning their foreign policy with the European Union, they have implemented restrictions matching the EU's comprehensive ban on RT broadcasting and distribution.

Australia

Partial restriction Since: February 2022 Reported Deadline

RT was never banned by law in Australia. SBS suspended its RT bulletins and Foxtel dropped the channel in February 2022, both carriage decisions by the broadcasters themselves. The website remains reachable.

Canada

Partial restriction Since: March 2022 In force CRTC (Canada)

Broadcasting Decision CRTC 2022-68 removed RT and RT France from the list of non-Canadian services authorised for distribution, so Canadian television providers may no longer carry them. The measure covers broadcast carriage only; rt.com itself is not blocked and OONI measurements from Canada record no interference.

Ukraine

Complete ban Since: August 2014 Reported Freedom House

Banned alongside other Russian state media after the annexation of Crimea and blocked at ISP level under sanctions decrees renewed since 2017. OONI recorded anomalies or confirmed blockpages in roughly 85% of Ukrainian measurements of rt.com.

United Kingdom

Complete ban Since: March 2022 In force Ofcom

Ofcom revoked RT's broadcast licence in March 2022. Separately, sanctions regulations in force since 29 April 2022 designate TV-Novosti and require internet access providers to take reasonable steps to block rt.com, on penalty of up to GBP 1m. Enforcement is uneven: the blocks are DNS-level, not every provider applies them, and no provider has faced action, so the site is still reachable on many networks.

United States of America

Partial restriction Since: March 2022 Reported Deadline

Major private providers dropped RT America in 2022, forcing it to cease operations. Furthermore, US tech giants have independently banned or restricted RT globally on their platforms. The US government also requires RT to register as a foreign agent under FARA.
