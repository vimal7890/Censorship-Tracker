Where is Viber blocked?

Viber is blocked, restricted or age-gated in 10 countries and territories — 6 complete bans and 4 partial restrictions in total.

See it in the interactive index →

Egypt

Partial restriction Since: Longstanding Reported Freedom House

Egypt does not impose a blanket ban on Viber, but authorities have repeatedly throttled or interfered with VoIP features on messaging apps including Viber and WhatsApp; text messaging generally works. Making international calls over unlicensed VoIP is technically illegal under Article 72 of Egypt's Telecommunication Regulation Law.

Eritrea

Complete ban Since: Ongoing Reported

Islamic Republic of Iran

Complete ban Since: 2015 In force UK Government

Islamic Republic of Iran blocks or filters Viber alongside other major foreign messaging services. The United Kingdom government's 2025 country-information note identifies Viber among the messaging apps blocked in Islamic Republic of Iran.

North Korea

Complete ban Since: Forever Reported Wikipedia

Oman

Partial restriction Since: Longstanding Reported Human Rights Watch

Oman restricts unlicensed VoIP services: Viber voice and video calls are blocked by local carriers under telecommunications licensing rules, while messaging still works. Human Rights Watch and other groups have long documented Oman's ban on consumer VoIP platforms alongside the United Arab Emirates and Qatar.

People's Republic of China

Complete ban Since: October 2014 Reported TechCrunch

Viber's messaging and calling service was blocked on mainland Chinese networks during the 2014 crackdown on foreign chat apps; it was reachable only through a VPN in contemporaneous testing.

Qatar

Partial restriction Since: Longstanding Reported Human Rights Watch

Qatar restricts unlicensed VoIP services: Viber voice and video calls are blocked on local networks, while messaging still works. The restriction is part of the same Gulf-region licensing regime that also blocks WhatsApp, Skype, and FaceTime calling.

Russia

Complete ban Since: December 2024 Reported The Record

Roskomnadzor blocked access to Viber in December 2024, citing the app's failure to comply with national legislation and its refusal to remove content regarding the war in Ukraine.

Turkmenistan

Complete ban Since: Longstanding Reported Radio Free Europe / Radio Liberty

Viber has long been inaccessible in Turkmenistan under the state's broad block on foreign messaging and social-media services.

United Arab Emirates

Partial restriction Since: 2013 Reported Freedom House

Viber's calling service remains blocked in the United Arab Emirates under the unlicensed-VoIP regime; Freedom House reports that the block has been in place since 2013. This is a feature restriction rather than a block of text messaging.
